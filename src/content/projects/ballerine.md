## The background

Ballerine operates at the intersection of compliance, fraud prevention and AI
infrastructure, a category that is evolving faster than most. Their platform
serves payment service providers, banks, fintechs and marketplaces, each with
their own risk workflows and regulatory pressures.

With a growing product suite covering everything from merchant underwriting to
agentic commerce, Ballerine needed a website that could keep pace with the
breadth of their offering and the speed at which it was expanding.

## The challenge

The old Ballerine website had grown into a bottleneck. Page speed was slipping
as the site expanded, the CMS structure could not easily handle the steady
stream of new products and use cases coming out of the roadmap, and even small
marketing updates had become development tasks. The site needed to match the
speed of the business, both literally and operationally.

## The solution

Ballerine came to inparallel with a fully realised design in Figma and a clear
goal: build it right, build it fast, and build it to last. The development side
ran in three parallel tracks.

First, a component library built before any pages went into production. Every
reusable piece of the design was set up once and used across all 30+ pages, with
one immediate result: new pages now ship in hours, not days, because the
building blocks are already there.

Second, a CMS architecture designed for the marketing team to manage
independently, with pages, products and resources structured so that adding a
new use case page would not require engineering involvement.

Third, performance baked in from the first line of code, with attention paid to
load times, image optimisation and Core Web Vitals at every stage of the build.

## The result

The impact was immediate and measurable. Page speed went from a bottleneck to a
baseline, with the new site loading fast across every page and every device. The
marketing team gained the autonomy they needed — new use case pages and product
pages now ship without engineering involvement, and what used to take weeks now
takes hours.

Most telling, average engagement time on the site grew 16x compared to the
previous version, with visitors spending significantly more time exploring
Ballerine's product surface.

## The achievement

- 30+ pages built in Webflow from a Figma design
- Reusable component library covering the full design system
- Scalable CMS architecture giving the marketing team full publishing autonomy
- Performance optimisation across load times, Core Web Vitals and image delivery
- 16x increase in average user engagement time compared to the previous site
- Faster turnaround on new product, use case and resource pages
- Three competitors reaching out to ask who built the site
