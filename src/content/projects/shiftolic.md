## The background

Shiftolic operates in a corner of healthcare that does not get much attention
until something goes wrong. Hospitals run short on physicians, agencies cannot
fill shifts fast enough, and burnout climbs every time someone has to work
back-to-back.

Their platform connects physicians, healthcare facilities and staffing agencies
through a marketplace that handles shift scheduling, credentialing and coverage
in one place. As the product grew, the website did not grow with it.

## The challenge

The old Shiftolic site no longer reflected what the platform had become. It
looked dated next to the product, the messaging did not match the current
positioning, and there was no easy way to tell new visitors what Shiftolic
actually does.

On top of that, the timeline was tight. Two weeks from kickoff to launch, design
and development both, with no room to push the deadline.

## The solution

inparallel took the project end to end. Design and development ran in parallel,
with each side moving in tight one-week sprints.

The first week was design: positioning, messaging structure, visual direction
and high-fidelity UI for every page. Two design revisions, both turned around
fast. The second week was development: the Webflow build, CMS architecture,
integrations and QA. Two development revisions, again turned around fast.

Every decision through the project was made with the timeline in mind —
components built once and reused across pages, structures kept simple where
simplicity worked, performance dialled in from the start so launch day would not
get held up by optimisation.

## The result

The site launched on time, exactly two weeks after kickoff. The new design
captured what Shiftolic had grown into, with clear positioning, modern visuals
and messaging that finally matched the product. The CMS was set up for the
marketing team to manage independently, and performance was solid from day one.

What started as a tight two-week project turned into an ongoing partnership
through inparallel+, with the crew continuing to handle new pages, updates and
improvements as Shiftolic keeps growing.

## The achievement

- Full website design and development delivered in 2 weeks
- Two design revisions and two development revisions, both turned around within the timeline
- Modern visual identity that finally matched the maturity of the product
- Messaging restructured to position Shiftolic clearly for hospitals, agencies and physicians
- Webflow build with a scalable CMS for marketing autonomy
- Performance optimised from the first line of code
- Ongoing partnership through inparallel+ supporting Shiftolic's continued growth
