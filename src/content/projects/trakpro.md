## The background

TrakPro is an Irish platform for subcontractor commercial management, bringing
payment claims, variations, dayworks, retention, contra charges and compliance
into one system instead of spreadsheets and email chains.

It is used by contractors including CBRE, Collen, Chas Berger and Comer Group
across Ireland, the United Kingdom, Sweden, Portugal and Germany, backed by
Enterprise Ireland and certified to ISO 27001.

The old site was static and basic. It carried none of that weight.

## The challenge

TrakPro sells into two markets that share a language and use different words for
the same document. In the United Kingdom a subcontractor submits a payment
application, under the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996.
In Ireland it is a payment claim, under the Construction Contracts Act 2013. Get
that wrong and the site reads as software built for someone else.

On top of it, a product that replaces an entire workflow had to be made obvious
to an enterprise audience, and the new site had to take over the old domain
without losing anything it had earned.

## The solution

TrakPro came to inparallel with a brief for a landing page and a product far
bigger than one. The work ran in three tracks.

First, design from scratch in Figma: competitor analysis, a full design system,
and custom illustration for the "Before TrakPro" section that carries the
argument on the home page.

Second, localisation. We audited the entire site against both acts before
touching Webflow Localization, found and fixed the inconsistencies in the base
copy, then built Ireland as a second locale where terminology switches on every
instance that carries legal meaning and stays put where it does not.

Third, the build: a reusable component structure, a CMS the marketing side runs
without us, a custom PDF viewer on PDF.js, a lazy-mounted booking widget,
analytics and cookie consent, and a complete redirect map through Webflow with a
Cloudflare fallback.

## The result

The numbers moved in every category that a search engine and an AI assistant
read first. SEO reached a perfect 100 on both mobile and desktop. Best Practices
reached 100 on mobile, with the third-party cookies from the Vimeo and Calendly
embeds handled rather than left to drag the score down.

On an independent audit, AI readiness went from the lowest grade on the old site
to the highest on the new one, and on-page SEO climbed the same way, from the
bottom of the scale to the top. Mobile performance rose almost 20%, and it did
so while the new site carries video, an interactive markets map and product
imagery the old three-page site never had.

Underneath the scores, the site now replaces the old one on the same domain with
a complete redirect map, so no URL it had earned was lost. Visitors in Ireland
and the United Kingdom read the site in the terminology their own contracts use.
The TrakPro team publishes their Toolbox Talks series through the CMS with no
developer involved.

## The achievement

- 23% fewer issues to fix on an independent audit, with AI readiness and on-page SEO both moving from the lowest grade to the highest
- From 3 indexable pages to a full multi-page site, all of it discoverable where the old site exposed almost nothing to a search
- Two markets served from one site in the terminology each law requires: payment applications in the United Kingdom under the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996, payment claims in Ireland under the Construction Contracts Act 2013, with reciprocal hreflang the old site never had
- A CMS the TrakPro team updates on their own, publishing and editing content with no developer involved
